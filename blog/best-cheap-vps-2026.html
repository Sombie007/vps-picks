How I pick a "worth buying" deal

  • Renewal price first. A $5 first year that renews at $40 is worse than $16 forever. Black Friday plans being lifetime recurring is exactly why they lead this list.
  • Real KVM, not containers. KVM means Docker works, kernels are yours, and neighbors can't eat your RAM.
  • Traffic quota honesty. 3–10 TB/month covers virtually every personal use case; anything beyond that is marketing.
  • Provider age. I only feature providers with years of operating history — the budget hosting graveyard is enormous.

What can you actually run on a $16/year VPS?

More than you'd think: a personal blog or portfolio, a WireGuard/jump server, uptime monitoring, cron jobs and scrapers, small APIs, a Git mirror, DNS toys. What it won't do: heavy databases with real traffic, video transcoding, or anything latency-critical. Match the plan to the job and a year of hosting costs less than a pizza.
